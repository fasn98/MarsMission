  • Payload mass greater than 10,000 kg (22,046 lbm) requires the standard service high-capacity 3169.9-mm (124.8-in) payload adapter; however, a mission-unique separation system or other accommodations may be required, which could have cost and/or performance impacts.
  • 2 payload fairing doors.
